Impact and Knowledge Exchange

Turning civil engineering research into applied practice

Research platform leadership, industry-facing delivery, and independently reported outcomes. This page summarises how my research has reached engineering practice, and what independent sources say about it.

Research platform leadership

Founding Dean of a nonprofit applied research academy in Chongqing, established with district and municipal science-and-technology support and built on research links with KU Leuven and other European institutions.

Academy website

Institution building

From a research idea to an operating platform

Named as the designated head of the Academy in the district government approval of March 2019, I led its platform-construction period: setting research directions, forming research centres, coordinating academic and enterprise partners, and communicating applied outcomes to non-specialist audiences.

2019-2023 construction periodnonprofit applied research

International cooperation

China-Europe research links in practice

The platform grew out of a science-and-technology cooperation agreement between Chongqing and KU Leuven. Its work included two-way researcher mobility, joint doctoral education with KU Leuven, and cooperation frameworks with European research groups in photonics-based sensing and micro-integration.

Pathways from research to practice

Selected routes by which my research methods have reached identifiable users in industry and public infrastructure. Corroborating statements from the organisations involved are available on request.

Digital quality assessment

Reality capture for facade quality evaluation

Laser scanning and model-based assessment methods developed in my published research were written into a project inspection procedure for a large commercial development, moving quality checking from sampling by hand to model-referenced evaluation.

published methodswritten procedurecommercial developer

Grid resilience

Wind-hazard resilience for transmission infrastructure

Principal investigator on commissioned research programmes for provincial and national grid organisations covering wind-disaster scenario reconstruction, intelligent risk assessment, and emergency repair decision support for transmission lines.

PIstate grid organisationsmulti-hazard

Structural monitoring

Sensor-based bridge and structure monitoring

MEMS sensing and BIM-linked monitoring work applied to bridges and public infrastructure, including UAV and sensing-based inspection with model-based interpretation for maintenance planning.

MEMS sensingbridge monitoringasset management

Sustainable construction

Construction-waste recycling in road maintenance

A recycled-materials pilot on an expressway section, drawing on European construction-waste recycling research, turning excavated maintenance waste into reusable material on site.

circular constructionfield pilot

Research training and incubation

The platform supported postgraduate and doctoral researchers, hosted international exchange students, and incubated technology ventures in sensing and digital construction, with three companies registered by core team members.
