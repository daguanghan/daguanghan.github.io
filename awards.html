Awards

Three First Prizes, two German research awards, and a national individual award

Three provincial- or ministerial-level First Prizes for science and technology progress, two research awards from Germany, and an individual national award for research work. Award names, issuing bodies and project titles are given so each can be checked.

Science and technology prizes

2024 | First Prize

Award for Scientific and Technological Innovation, China Association for the Promotion of Industrial Development of Science and Technology

For Key Technologies and Applications for Structural Safety Diagnostics and Smart Disaster Prevention in Civil Infrastructure.

2022 | First Prize

Science and Technology Progress Award, China Highway and Transportation Society

For Key Technologies for Precise Diagnosis and Prevention of Fatigue Damage under Multi-factor Coupling Effects in Long-Span Bridges.

2016 | First Prize

Science and Technology Progress Award, Ministry of Education of the People’s Republic of China

For Key Technology and Engineering Application for Structural Capacity Enhancement of As-Built Structures — the strengthening and retrofit line that still runs through the current research agenda.

Individual research awards

Awarded to a named researcher rather than to a project team.

2014 | National

Outstanding Scientific Researcher Award, Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security, People’s Republic of China

2009–2011 | Germany

Outstanding Scientific Researcher Award, Universität der Bundeswehr München

Awarded during doctoral research at the university where I took my Dr.-Ing.

2010 | Germany

Best Exhibition Award, World Congress of Surveying

For Research on Deformation Performance of Concrete Structures by 3D Laser Scanning — the earliest public recognition of the reality-capture line that later became the most-cited part of my work.

2015 | Industry

Excellence Award for Outstanding Young Researchers, China Metallurgical Group (MCC)

2014 | Industry

Young Talent Award for Science and Technology Innovation, China Metallurgical Group (MCC)

For Algorithm Research on Reinforced-Concrete Surface Cracks Based on 3D Laser Scanning.
